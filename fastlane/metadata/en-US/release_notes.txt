New: Favorites
- Save fixed combinations of customer, project and activity as favorites and start them with one tap
- Favorites start a timer or book a fixed duration, either until now or from now (for example 10 minutes for a call that just ended)
- New Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: several favorites at a glance, a single favorite, or "Choose duration"
- Quick access: start favorites by touching and holding the app icon, by touching and holding "Start Timer", from Spotlight and with Siri
- Favorites on Apple Watch too
- A running timer is never replaced without asking
- Create favorites right in Settings → Favorites and manage them there: reorder, rename, change the duration
